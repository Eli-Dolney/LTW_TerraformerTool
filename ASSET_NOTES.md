# Asset Notes

The two item textures are original assets created for LTW Terraformer Tool with OpenAI's built-in image generation, then cropped, background-cleared, and downscaled to transparent 32×32 PNGs for Minecraft.

## Survival Builder Wand

Prompt direction: “A single Minecraft-style pixel-art survival builder wand, readable at item-icon size, sturdy wooden and iron construction with subtle cyan magical accents, diagonal composition, crisp square pixels, no text, no border, isolated on a flat green chroma background.”

Final asset: `src/main/resources/assets/ltwterraformer/textures/item/survival_builder_wand.png`

## Ultimate Creative Terraformer

Prompt direction: “A single Minecraft-style pixel-art ultimate terraformer tool, powerful arcane-tech wand with a dark handle, glowing cyan/teal energy core and gold details, diagonal composition, crisp square pixels, no text, no border, isolated on a flat green chroma background.”

Final asset: `src/main/resources/assets/ltwterraformer/textures/item/creative_terraformer.png`
